# The 38 Clans of Ferrest #
The tribe of Ferrest is subdivided into 38 clans, which are social units that all claim ancestry from a common ancestor, typically a paternal ancestor but there are some clans that claim both a mother and a father as their original ancestors. In there past, there have existed many other clans who no longer exist, typically not because they were wiped out, but because their numbers decreased to the point that they were no longer a viable clan, so they merged with a related clan and adopted the larger clan's name.

These clans do not all claim their ancestry directly from the historical person of Ferrest, though they all do bear his ancestry do to mixing. Rather, they trace ancestry from six different sources. Ferrest, Lrike, Kelmdri, Duhes Raskine, Objaurtiwo Jelmakne, and the Dzemali. The Ferrestans trace their ancestry back directly to a man named Ferrest, who was a direct descendant of Hulre Oskutran via one of his wives rather than concubines. Lrike was a close relative of Ferrest, who's descendants aligned themselves with Ferrest's and merged with his. Kelmdri was a another descendant of Hulre Oskutran, whose descendants formed their own distinct tribal identity and only later became united with Ferrest. Duhes Raskine was a friend and relative of Ferrest, the patriarch of the Duhas tribe. According to Lenošten oral and later written history, he was the leader of the Lenošten army that destroyed the Ulankolid empire. He wound up in a situation where he wanted to marry the daughter of Ferrest, but Ferrest, upon his daughter's urging, refused to giver her. Duhas resolved to take her by force and launched a raid on Ferrest's settlement. During the raid, Ferrest was killed. Duhas was overcome by shame and remorse for what he had done, and returned all the captives. He also, according to the laws that he had begun to lay down, gave himself to be a servant of Ferrest's descendants for a few decades. though Ferrest's tribe refused to keep him for that time, they did adopt one of his sons as a replacement. Objaurtiwo Jelmakne are the descendants of people who are not Lenošten but joined with the Ferrestans either in the mountains of mainland Srokeskia or in Preohlednia. The Dzemali ancestry comes from clans of Dzemali who joined with the Ferrestans
