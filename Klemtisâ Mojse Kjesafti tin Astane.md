# Klemtisâ Mojse Kjesafti tin Astane #

Klemtisâ was born in the Berdason range in the village of Stjaklajn, located on the Almandu side at an elevation of around 4200 feet. Stjaklajn, at this point, had been a village for over 250 years, and had a winter population of 248. At the age of seven, he moved with his family to Tjatse Kosno, where his father moved to shore up the defenses of that very strategic location. Growing up, he was considered quite woodcrafty and a competent warrior, but hit true love was for boats and sailing, and for the Swenlask his village overlooked. He completed the rite of passage to adulthood at the rather young age of 15, and soon found work upon the ships of Kose Karti, sailing to and fro upon the Swenlask bearing goods and passengers. 

At the time of the death of Kropuhes III, Klemtisâ was on shore leave in Dohmulo. Since his clan was closely allied with tin Albas, Klemtisâ and a few other members of his clan gathered with the other supporters of Lekjos tin Albas in the main market of Cernit. Lekjos went to the roof of a 3 story building to give a speach to rile up his supporters against Kjorkos tin Ferrest. Meanwhile, Kjorkos took some soldiers of Kose Karti and of his own clan, went up onto the building, seized him, ran him through with a spear, and cast his dying body from the roof. Then the soldiers went down into the crowd to disperse it, weapons drawn to deal with anyone who resisted. The crowd scattered into the streets of the town, including Klemtisâ. A bit later that day, he ran into three soldiers, one of Ferrest and the other two of Kose Karti. In a moment of panic, he drew his weapon and struck the nearest soldier, one of Kose Karti, slicing through his arm to the bone. Realizing his mistake, Klemtisâ tried to flee back towards his ship, but even though he was able to make an escape, he suffered an injury to his shoulder.

Klemtisâ realized that he could not return to his ship, as the Kose Karti captain and crew would not forgive his actions that day, and the word was already being spread to search for a young member of the tin Astane clan with an injured shoulder. Thus, Klemtisâ decided to flee over the mountains, and find work on the ships of the sea, away from Ferrest.
