# Translation of Passage

## Source Text

> Jeswos un kmit o jekto ", skjo dajkê, a njet majnsers ktats sinatlo kwom u na?"
>
> Kjan u it ", kno du was Jawjos Anni doms kolkmat mandoms sajtô mandoms dlirsko. Je wu jims man mejns ktats sinatlo. Wins ihu. Kno du was o lwosk musk, skantiwe seucisu was kno u na. Biswam u jik bjats nejt wins na."
>
> Re jestam fu bfolo stu jomad os stjeki Annjot. Kmum face jom, jemu alkisuj sbari os lo ot was Annjot.

## Sentence-by-Sentence Analysis

### Sentence 1

> **Jeswos un kmit o jekto ", skjo dajkê, a njet majnsers ktats sinatlo kwom u na?"**

| Word | Analysis | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| *Jeswos* | Proper name; possibly contains *jes* (deer) + *wos* (cf. *woski* "cousin/clan member") | Jeswos (a personal name) |
| *un* | Verb particle: past tense simple | [past] |
| *kmit* | **New.** Verb root. From context (past tense, with a speech-introducing clause following): "asked" or "said" or "questioned" | asked / questioned |
| *o* | Definite singular animate nominative article | the |
| *jekto* | **New.** Noun, animate. Possibly related to *ekto* "crowd, club, mob, faction" — with a prefix *j-*. Or a standalone noun meaning "elder," "priest," "leader" | elder / leader (of a group?) |
| *skjo* | **New.** Possibly a vocative particle or an interjection introducing direct address. Could be related to *kjo*? Or a contraction. | O / (vocative marker) |
| *dajkê* | *daj* "wise" + *-kê* (animate noun suffix) = "wise one / sage" (cf. the *dajkê* paradigm in the grammar section) | wise one |
| *a* | Definite singular inanimate nominative article | the |
| *njet* | **New.** Inanimate noun. Possibly "answer," "truth," "reason," or "meaning" — it is the thing being asked about | answer / reason / truth |
| *majnsers* | **New.** Likely a compound or derived form. *man* "and" + possibly a root. Or: related to *mejns* (which appears in the next paragraph). Context suggests this is an adjective or noun meaning "great" or "true" or "righteous" | great / true / righteous |
| *ktats* | *kt-* "in/within" + *ats* (def. sg. inan. accusative) = "in the [thing]" | in the |
| *sinatlo* | **New.** Inanimate noun. *sinat-* + *-lo* (cf. *lo* "pasture/field/owned territory"). If *sinat-* relates to *asinte/asintê* "memory/memory-keeper," this could mean "the field/domain of memory" — i.e., "tradition," "the lore," or "the law as remembered." Or *sinat-* could be an independent root. | law / tradition / the lore |
| *kwom* | *kw-* "before (in front of)" + *om* — possibly *oms* (def. sg. animate comitative?) or a reduced form. "Before" or "in front of" something/someone | before / in the presence of |
| *u* | Verb particle: present tense simple | [present] |
| *na* | "what" (interrogative) | what |

**Translation:**

> Jeswos asked the elder: "O wise one, what is the answer/truth of the great [things] in the law/tradition, before [us], what is it?"

**Smoother English:**

> **Jeswos asked the elder: "Wise one, what is the great truth within the law?"**

| Word | Analysis | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| *Biswam* | **New.** Possibly a compound: *bis-* + *wam*? Or *bj-* "on/upon" + *swam*? Or a single word meaning "upon these" or "on these two" | On these (two) / Upon these |

### Sentence 3

> **Re jestam fu bfolo stu jomad os stjeki Annjot.**

| Word | Analysis | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| *Re* | **New.** Possibly *r-* "because" + *e* (indef. sg. animate) = "because a/an..." Or a conjunction: "for" / "because" | For / Because |
| *jestam* | *j-* (opposite?) + *estam* "all" = "every single" or "each one"? Or simply *estam* "all" with a prefix | every / all |
| *fu* | *f-* (perfective) + *u* (present) = perfective present: "has [done]" | has |
| *bfolo* | "a crime, a breach of boundaries, a breach of trust" | transgressed / committed a crime |
| *stu* | *st-* "not" + *u* (present) = negation particle, or here the negation prefix used adverbially: "not" / "against" | against / in violation of |
| *jomad* | **New.** *jom* "we" + *-ad*? Or a separate word. Possibly from *jom* genitive? But that's *jomê*. Or: *jom-* + *-ad* = "our" in some construction. Could also be a noun. | our / ours — or: "the standard" |
| *os* | Def. sg. animate accusative/dative article: could be "the" (acc.) | the |

**Translation:** "For all have transgressed against the laws/commandments of God."

> **Kmum face jom, jemu alkisuj sbari os lo ot was Annjot.**

| Word | Analysis | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| *Kmum* | *km-* "certainly" + *um* (future tense) = "will certainly" | will certainly |
| *face* | "to die" | die |
| *jom* | 1st person plural absolutive: "we" | we |
| *jemu* | 1st person plural ergative: "we (as agents)" | we (who) |
| *alkisuj* | *alk-* "from" + *isuj* — *is-* (participial) + *uj* (present continuous) = "from [being those who are] continuously..." i.e., "having been removed from" or "being cast from" | being cast from / separated from |
| *sbari* | **New.** Likely a noun or verb: "to enter," "to dwell," "to partake in." Or: related to *sbonten* "forest"? In context, this is what we are separated from — likely "dwelling" or "resting" or "the presence (of)" | dwelling in / the presence of |
| *os* | Def. sg. animate accusative/dative: "the" (direction of dative?) | the |
| *lo* | "pasture, hunting ground, field, or fishery owned by an individual or clan" — here used metaphorically: "the domain / the territory" | domain / territory / garden |
| *ot* | Def. sg. animate genitive: "of the" | of the |
| *was* | "love, care, nourishment" — here as a noun via the article *ot*: "of the love/care" | love |
| *Annjot* | "of God" (same form as above) | of God |

**Translation:** "We will certainly die, we who are being separated from [dwelling in] the domain of God's love."

## Full Translation

> **Jeswos asked the elder: "Wise one, what is the great truth within the law?"**
>
> **He spoke: "You must love Jawjos, God, with your whole heart, and with your mind, and with your strength. This is the first and greatest [commandment] in the law. The second is like it: you must love your neighbor as you love yourselves. On these two all of the law stands."**
>
> **For all have transgressed against the commandments of God. We will certainly die, we who are being separated from dwelling in the domain of God's love.**

---

## Identification of the Source Material

This passage is recognizably a Ferrestan rendering of **Matthew 22:36–40** (the Greatest Commandment) combined with a theological statement echoing **Romans 3:23** and **Romans 6:23**. The first paragraph is a question about the greatest commandment in the law. The response — love God with heart, mind, and strength; love your neighbor as yourself; on these two hang all the law — is the standard Synoptic Gospel formulation. The final paragraph adds a Pauline gloss about universal transgression and the consequence of death/separation from God's love.

This is strong evidence that this passage comes from a **Ferrestan Christian context** — likely Orthodox or Protestant — and may be part of a catechism, a sermon, or a Ferrestan Bible translation. The theological vocabulary (*Annjot* "of God," *sinatlo* "the law/tradition," *stjeki* "commandments," *kolkmat* "whole heart/gut-feeling") represents a religious register of Ferrestan that would have developed through centuries of Christian presence on the island.
